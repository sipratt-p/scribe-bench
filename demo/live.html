Live decision support during the visit

The recording plays as if the visit were happening now. The streaming transcript arrives on the audio timeline, and every few seconds a local model updates a view meant to change what the clinician does before the patient leaves: a working differential with the evidence for each and what is still missing, the one question that best separates the top two, act-now red flags, safety-netting not yet given, and guideline-based plan suggestions kept apart from what the clinician actually said. When the visit ends, the live timeline is scored against the clinician's own note: how early the right diagnosis appeared, whether the suggested questions got asked, red-flag precision, and whether the plan suggestions agreed with or contradicted the plan.

Suggestions, not decisions. Everything in the amber column is generated by a local model from a partial, unlabelled, sometimes misheard transcript; it is here to be measured, not trusted.
